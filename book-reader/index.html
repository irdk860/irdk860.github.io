Install it on your phone or computer

Book Reader is a web app: install it once and it gets its own icon, opens full screen and keeps working offline.

iPhone or iPad

  1. Open this page in Safari.
  2. Tap the Share button (the square with an arrow pointing up).
  3. Scroll down and tap Add to Home Screen, then Add.
  4. Open Book Reader from your home screen. Your PDFs are in the Files app when you tap the cover.

Android

  1. Open this page in Chrome.
  2. Tap the ⋮ menu, then Add to Home screen or Install app.
  3. Open it from your home screen or app drawer.

Computer

  1. In Chrome or Edge, click the install icon at the right of the address bar.
  2. Or download the single file and open it in any browser.
